📖 HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Solo Quest! Get fit in real life to level up your character in the game.

1️⃣ Update Your Body Stats

Go to the PLAYER tab and enter your Height and Weight. The game will create a safe, custom workout plan just for you!

2️⃣ Do Your Daily Quests

Go to the QUEST tab. Prop your phone up, step back, and start moving! The camera will automatically count your reps. Complete all daily workouts for a massive reward.

3️⃣ Upgrade Your Hero

Leveling up gives you 5 Stat Points to spend:

  • ❤️ HP (Health): Take more hits.
  • ⚔️ ATK (Damage): Defeat enemies faster.
  • 🛡️ ARM (Armor): Block incoming damage.

Note: The max level is 100! After that, you earn Stat Points directly from quests instead of XP.

4️⃣ Conquer the Tower

Fight monsters in the TOWER tab. Bosses have special glowing shields you must break!

⚡ Speed Multiplier (1X to 5X): Click the ⚡ SPEED button on the left side of the arena to accelerate combat! Speed automatically resets to 1X after completing a floor.

📷 Camera Tips for Perfect Tracking:

  • ☀️ Make sure your room is bright!
  • 🚶 Stand far enough back so your whole body is inside the on-screen box.
  • 📱 For pushups, place your phone on the floor resting against a wall.
